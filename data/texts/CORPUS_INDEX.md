## Known Issues and Limitations

### 1. VALID_LANGUAGES does not include `de` (German)
**File**: `src/geometry_of_meaning/data.py`, line 18  
**Impact**: `OriginalText.__post_init__` and `Translation.__post_init__` reject language codes outside `{en, it, zh, ja, da}`. Two originals (`einstein_special_relativity`, `metamorphosis_opening`) have `original_language: "de"` and 10 translations have `source_language: "de"`. These will fail if loaded through the dataclass constructors, even though the files are correct.  
**Recommendation**: Expand `VALID_LANGUAGES` to `{"en", "it", "zh", "ja", "da", "de"}` or distinguish between "corpus source languages" and "experiment comparison languages".

### 2. All non-English translations are model-generated
All translations into it, zh, ja, da are AI-generated (Claude Sonnet 4) and marked `validation_status: "unreviewed"`. Human review by native speakers is required before these can be considered canonical.

### 3. English translations of German originals
The English translations of Einstein and Kafka were generated by model and aligned with published standard translations where possible (Beck translation for Einstein; Muir & Muir tradition for Kafka). They require human verification against specific authoritative translations.

### 4. Source text verification
Text passages were reconstructed from model knowledge, not from digitized sources. While these are widely-known passages with stable texts, human verification against specific edition page scans or Project Gutenberg texts is recommended before publication.
